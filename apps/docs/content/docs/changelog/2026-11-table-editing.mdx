### Tables

`addTable` inserts a table. Click into a cell to edit it; Tab and Shift+Tab move between cells, and Tab on the last cell adds a row. Drag a border to resize a row or column, and drag across cells or Shift+click to select a range to format, merge, clear, or copy. Pasting tab-separated text fills cells.

### Table operations

- `insertTableRow`, `deleteTableRow`, `insertTableColumn`, and `deleteTableColumn` change rows and columns, and `setTableGrid` sizes them.
- `mergeTableCells` and `splitTableCell` merge and split cells.
- `setTableCellStyle` sets cell fill, borders, and vertical alignment.
- `setTableOptions` toggles the header row, banded rows, and other table style parts.
- The text operations take a `cell` to edit one table cell.

`useSelection` adds `insertTable`, `cell`, `cellRange`, `changeTable`, and `setTableCellStyle`.
