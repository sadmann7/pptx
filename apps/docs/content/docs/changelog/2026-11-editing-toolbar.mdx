### Formatting toolbar

`useSelection` reads the selection and its text formatting, formats text and paragraphs, and inserts, duplicates, deletes, and reorders text boxes and shapes. Draw tools (`startDrawing`) place a shape where the slide is clicked or dragged, and `anchor` positions a floating toolbar over the selection.

[Read the docs](/docs/editing#formatting-toolbar)

### PowerPoint-style editing

- Snapping with guides (`snapping`, `snapThreshold`, Alt to place freely), rotation, and resizing rotated shapes.
- Copy and paste, arrange and duplicate shortcuts, and Tab between shapes with screen reader announcements.
- A click selects a text box; click again, double-click, or type to edit it.
- The overlay is composable from `SelectionBox`, `ResizeHandle`, `RotateHandle`, `SnapGuide`, and `Marquee`.

### Edit operations

The core adds `setTextStyle`, `setParagraphStyle`, `setOutline`, `addSlide`, `setSlideBackground`, `addTextBox`, `addShape`, `addImage`, `duplicateNode`, `reorderNode`, and `pasteNodes`. `getPresentationFonts()` and `getPresetShapePath()` feed font and shape pickers.

[Read the docs](/docs/editing#programmatic-edits)

### Fixes

- Themes nested in another folder load, instead of rendering theme colors black.
- The thumbnail list keeps at most 50 rendered miniatures, so memory stays flat on long decks.
- Edits, undo, and redo run one at a time, and `undo()` returns a promise like `redo()`.
