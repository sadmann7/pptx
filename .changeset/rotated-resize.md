Resize rotated shapes along their own axes when selected alone, keeping the edge opposite the handle in place, with resize cursors that turn with the shape. Snapping and marquee selection use rotated shapes' on-slide bounds. Right-clicking a shape outside the selection now selects it, and right-clicking empty canvas clears the selection. The rotate cursor now points clockwise from the top.
